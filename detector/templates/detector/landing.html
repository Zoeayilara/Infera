What makes us different

Four-layer detection engine

Most tools check one signal. Infera fuses four independent modalities so even sophisticated phishing attempts are caught.

Text embedding analysis

Detects urgency language, credential requests, and phishing keyword patterns in the email body and subject — 40% of the final score.

URL feature extraction

Analyses every link — domain spoofing, suspicious TLDs, IP addresses as URLs, brand impersonation like paypa1.net vs paypal.com.

Sender metadata scoring

Scores the sender's domain for typosquatting, suspicious TLDs, and impersonation of known brands like Microsoft, Apple, or your bank.

Attachment scanning

Flags dangerous file types (.exe, .vbs, .bat), suspicious Office documents with potential macros, and phishing-named PDF attachments.
