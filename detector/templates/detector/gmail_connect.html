Infera connects via IMAP and scans both your Inbox and Spam folder — because most real phishing emails are already in Spam. Your actual password is never used.

Infera scans two folders automatically:
📥 Inbox — regular emails (safe + any phishing Gmail missed)
🚨 Spam folder — where most real phishing lives, already flagged by Gmail
This means even if your inbox looks all safe, your spam folder will contain the real threats that Infera's AI will classify and explain to you with full analysis.
How to get your Gmail App Password (one-time setup)
1
Enable 2-Step Verification
Go to myaccount.google.com/security and turn on 2-Step Verification. App Passwords only appear after this is enabled.
2
Enable IMAP in Gmail settings
In Gmail → Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Enable IMAP → Save Changes. Required for spam folder access.
3
Create an App Password
Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Type Infera → click Create. Google gives you a 16-character password like: abcd efgh ijkl mnop
4
Paste it below and connect
Enter your Gmail address and that 16-character App Password below. Infera will immediately scan your Inbox and Spam folder.
